AURUM is designed as a concept for creators, educators and experts who want to turn their knowledge into a structured digital offer.

The framework starts with fundamentals and gradually moves toward product, content, conversion and delivery.

The concept contains six chapters, each focused on one part of the creator-business system.

A real implementation could add new modules, lessons, resources, community features and supporting products over time.
